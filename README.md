<h2>👨🏻‍💻 Hi, I'm Mark</h2>

> Anyone can become great at anything with the right teacher, and that's what I aim to be!

I'm a Ph.D. Candidate, University Lecturer, and Software Engineer based in Maynooth, Co. Kildare, Ireland. I have lectured Computer Science at **[Maynooth University](https://www.maynoothuniversity.ie/)** (CS230, CS615, CS618, CS620), and previously lectured Android App Development at **[Dublin City University](https://www.dcu.ie/)** and taught AI/ML and Mobile App Development at **[Fuzhou University](https://www.fzu.edu.cn/)**. Formerly a UI/UX Engineer at **[SAP](https://www.sap.com/uk/index.html)**.

I'm pursuing a Ph.D. in Generative AI and Collaborative Education Technologies at Maynooth University's Computer Science Department, supervised by Professor Dr. John G. Keating, and funded by the John & Pat Hume Scholarship. 

My thesis, *"Classrooms, Collaborations and Chatbots: Investigating the Use of Generative AI Agents in Collaborative Education in Higher Education,"* is expected to be completed in 2026. I graduated from Maynooth University with a First Class Honours B.Sc. in Computer Science & Software Engineering in 2022 (thesis: *"SPARSE: Collaborative AR Environments for Digital Learning,"* supervised by Dr. Ralf Bierig).

## 🔬 Research Focus

My research sits at the intersection of **Education Technology, Large Language Models, and the critical use of Generative AI (GenAI) in Higher Education**.

- **Key Goals:** Establishing best practices, creating tools, and developing guidelines for integrating GenAI into teaching and assessment.
- **Collaborators:** University of Rome Tor Vergata (Digital Learning Lab) and University of Hong Kong (CITE).
